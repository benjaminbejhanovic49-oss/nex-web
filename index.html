Do you work with international clients?

Yes. NEX STUDIO works remotely with businesses worldwide, and project communication is in English.

Can I start with a single landing page?

Absolutely. We can start with one focused page and agree on an appropriate scope for your offer or campaign.

How much does a project cost?

Projects are quoted individually based on their size, features and design requirements. Send us a short brief to discuss the scope.

What should I send in my first email?

Your business name, what you would like to build or improve, a preferred timeline and any relevant links or inspiration.
